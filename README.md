## ▪️ About me

I'm a **Software Quality Engineer (QA)** working in agile, cross-functional teams. My mission is to make sure digital products meet high standards of quality, performance and user experience.

I have solid experience in requirements analysis, manual and automated testing, bug management and technical documentation. I also work closely with Product, UX and Engineering on scope definition and delivery prioritization. Before QA, I worked as a developer (C#/.NET, React, TypeScript, Node.js), which helps me understand the code I test.

**What I do in practice**

- 📋 Analyze functional and business requirements, keeping them clear and traceable from conception to delivery
- 🧪 Create and run manual and automated test cases, focused on efficient coverage and risk-based testing
- 🐞 Report, document and follow bugs until resolution
- 📝 Write technical documentation that supports communication between teams
- 🤝 Work with Product, UX and Engineering to ship consistent, user-focused deliveries
- 🔎 Keep looking for new tools, practices and strategies to improve quality processes
